## Brand & Style

This design system targets C-level executives, VP of Supply Chain, Headhunters, and global procurement boards. It communicates uncompromising rigor, strategic acumen, multi-million dollar fiscal discipline, and institutional trustworthiness.

The aesthetic philosophy balances **Executive Minimalism** with **Refined Frosted Glassmorphism**. The interface avoids decorative fluff; every visual cue reflects structure, compliance, calculated negotiations, and verified ROI. Translucent layers mimic corporate boardroom glazing, paired with sharp typographic precision, whisper-soft atmospheric shadows, and brushed-gold/amber accents that signal elite performance benchmarks and verified savings metrics.

### Key Visual Tenets
- **Boardroom Gravitas:** Anchored by deep, authoritative navy grounds and architectural slate structural dividers.
- **Controlled Translucency:** Selective glassmorphism applied to foreground summary cards, executive summaries, and floating KPI metrics, avoiding visual noise.
- **Value Realization Cues:** Refined amber and warm gold tokens reserved exclusively for financial impact figures, contract scale achievements, and tier-1 vendor partnerships.

## Colors

The palette leverages a focused, executive dark-mode canvas that emphasizes structural clarity and data density while reducing fatigue during deep portfolio evaluations.

- **Primary Canvas & Depth (`#0B192C`):** Midnight Navy forms the foundation of all canvases, modals, and deep elevation stages.
- **Structural Elevation & Surfaces (`#1E3E62`):** Slate Navy serves as structural surfaces, containers, card foundations, interactive hover states, and layered panel dividers.
- **Accent of Value & Performance (`#F1B82D`):** Refined Amber Gold is applied surgically to strategic metric badges (Savings %, Spend Managed, P&L optimizations), active tabs, and primary action buttons.
- **Clarity & High-Fidelity Text (`#F5F7FA`):** Pearl Gray delivers soft contrast without the stark harshness of pure white, functioning as the chief neutral for readable prose, headings, and high-priority figures.
- **Secondary Neutral Support (`#94A3B8`):** Muted slate neutral used for metadata, category labels, contract dates, and inactive navigation items.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric authority and open humanist forms of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for titles, case-study narratives, and executive overviews, with the structural calibration of **Inter** for dense transactional metrics, KPI arrays, spend analytics tables, and categorization tags.

### Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Numeric & Metric Dominance:** Key achievements (e.g., "$42M Spend", "18.4% Savings") utilize `kpi-stat` in tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict visual alignment across case studies.
- **Label Capitalization:** `label-sm` is consistently rendered in uppercase with wide letter-spacing (`0.06em`) when indexing strategic sourcing pillars, ESG indices, and category tags (e.g., CAPEX, DIRECT MATERIALS, LOGISTICS).
- **Prose Restraint:** Narrative summaries enforce comfortable reading measure (60–75 characters per line) using `body-md` in `Pearl Gray / 85%` opacity over dark backgrounds to eliminate reading fatigue.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a 12-column dynamic fluid grid for desktop and tablet viewports, collapsing gracefully to a unified 4-column structure on mobile devices.

### Viewport Breakpoints & Reflow
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max layout container capped at 1280px. 12 columns with 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters and generous 48px (`3rem`) screen margins. Side-by-side executive tenure timelines and metric heatmaps.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns with 20px gutters. Strategic sourcing case studies transition from 3-up to 2-up grids.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns with 16px (`1rem`) gutters and 20px (`1.25rem`) margins. Timeline tracks shift to single-column vertical flow with direct inline KPI cards.

### Spatial Rhythms
Element gaps respect a mathematical base-8 rhythm:
- Use `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) for badge interiors, icon-label locks, and dense KPI comparisons.
- Use `space-md` (16px) for item cards, form inputs, and stacked executive bullet points.
- Use `space-lg` (24px) for card body internal padding and intra-module grouping.
- Use `space-xl` (40px) between distinct contextual portfolio sections (e.g., separating "Contract Highlights" from "ERP Systems & Governance").

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established through multi-layer frosted glass substrates, subtle specular light-border highlights, and deep oceanic tinted drop-shadows.

### Glassmorphism & Tonal Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas:** Solid `#0B192C` with subtle radial gradient illumination at the viewport top (`rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.3)`).
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Card & Module Foundation):** `rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.45)` with a backdrop blur of `16px`, bounded by a perimeter border of `1px solid rgba(245, 247, 250, 0.08)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Hover & Active Overlays):** `rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.70)` with backdrop blur of `24px` and upper-edge highlight border `1px solid rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.25)`.
- **Surface Elevation 3 (Floating Metrics & Modals):** `rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.85)` with backdrop blur of `32px` and an ambient shadow: `0 20px 40px -10px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.5)`.

### Specular & Ambient Shadows
Standard black shadows look muddy over deep navy; all elevation states must tint drop shadows with navy undertones:
- **Low (Resting Cards):** `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(3, 9, 18, 0.35)`
- **Interactive / Hover:** `0 12px 28px -4px rgba(3, 9, 18, 0.5), 0 0 1px 1px rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.15)`

## Shapes

The shape system adopts standard curvature (`roundedness: 2`), introducing tailored micro-radii to balance enterprise precision with modern, touchable polish.

- **Micro Radii (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Applied to data table row selectors, contract status dots, and inline KPI indicators.
- **Component Radii (`0.5rem` / 8px):** The default for buttons, text fields, search selectors, and metric pills.
- **Card & Surface Radii (`1rem` / 16px):** Strategic sourcing modules, CV career timeline entries, and vendor evaluation cards.
- **Floating Modals & Hero Surfaces (`1.5rem` / 24px):** Used for executive summary overlays and full-screen project previews.
- **Pill Boundaries (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for micro performance badges (e.g., `+14.2% YoY`, `LEAN SIX SIGMA`, `SAP Ariba Certified`).

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Executive Action (e.g., "Download Confidential Dossier / CV", "Contact"):**
  - Background: Gradient from `#F1B82D` to `#DDA01E`. Text: `#0B192C` (Weight: 600).
  - Hover: Subtle scale `1.02` with an ambient glow `0 0 20px rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.35)`.
  - Padding: `0.75rem 1.5rem`, Border radius: `0.5rem`.
- **Secondary (e.g., "Review Case Study"):**
  - Background: `rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.4)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 247, 250, 0.15)`, Text: `#F5F7FA`.
  - Hover: Background `rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.8)`, Border color: `rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.5)`.

### 2. Badges & KPI Chips (Savings & Metrics)
- **Metallic Savings Badge:** 
  - Substrate: Linear gradient at 135deg across `rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.12)` to `rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.03)`.
  - Border: `1px solid rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.4)`. Text: `#F1B82D`.
  - Iconography: Precision trend arrow or currency indicator with gold glow.
- **Category & Taxonomy Chips (e.g., "OPEX Reduction", "RFP Lead"):**
  - Substrate: `rgba(245, 247, 250, 0.05)`, Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 247, 250, 0.1)`, Text: `#94A3B8`.

### 3. Cards & Case Studies
- Built using Frosted Glass Surface 1 (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`).
- Distinct header section holding corporate logo / company tier, tenure range, and primary spend managed.
- Subtle inner top-edge highlight using `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(245, 247, 250, 0.1)`.
- Interactive hover lifts the card translateY by `-3px` while shifting border highlight from neutral to warm amber-gold tint.

### 4. Career Timeline & Lists
- Minimal vertical track rendered in `rgba(30, 62, 98, 0.8)` with node points highlighted by dual-ring nodes (Inner: `#F1B82D`, Outer ring: `rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.2)`).
- List items feature customized bullet points: miniature diamond or chevron geometric marks in amber instead of default circular discs.

### 5. Input Fields & Search (Vendor & Project Filters)
- Field Surface: `rgba(11, 25, 44, 0.6)` inset into container.
- Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 247, 250, 0.12)`.
- Focus State: Border transitions to `#F1B82D` with focus-ring outline `0 0 0 2px rgba(241, 184, 45, 0.2)`. Text: `#F5F7FA`.

### 6. Procurement-Specific Modules
- **Spend & Impact Matrix:** Tabular micro-dashboards with embedded progress tracks (`#1E3E62` background track with `#F1B82D` achievement fill).
- **Governance & System Badges:** Monochromatic corporate integration pills displaying ERP mastery (SAP S/4HANA, Coupa, Oracle SCM) with subtle etched metallic borders.
